# Project scope

## Working title

**Multimodal Travel-Time Accessibility to Protection Services for Women in the Brazilian Amazon: An Intra-Municipal Equity Framework**

The title remains provisional. “Intra-municipal” will be retained only if the final origin data support within-municipality analysis.

## Motivation

Municipal service presence does not imply effective territorial access. In Pará, rural and riverine populations may depend on walking, roads, rivers, transfers, schedules, and seasonal operating conditions. Municipal averages and administrative presence indicators can therefore conceal female populations facing extreme travel burdens.

## Object of study

Spatial and operational accessibility of the female population, from intra-municipal origins, to relevant health, social assistance, public security, justice, specialized protection, and shelter services.

## General objective

Develop a spatial intra-municipal equity framework to estimate and analyze door-to-door multimodal travel-time accessibility of the female population to VAW protection and response services in Pará, considering territorial, operational, and seasonal differences.

## Core travel-time model

`T_total = T_access + T_waiting + T_in_vehicle + T_transfer + T_egress`

## Analytical principles

1. Represent populated origins within municipalities.
2. Weight accessibility metrics by female population.
3. Distinguish service categories and functions.
4. Model walking, road, river, and defensible intermodal connections.
5. Treat air transport only in justified scenarios.
6. Separate theoretical minimum time from operational expected time.
7. Represent flood and dry seasons when evidence supports parameterization.
8. Expose uncertainty and validate calculated times.
9. Preserve reproducibility and data provenance.

## Exclusions

This project does not estimate violence incidence, individual risk, underreporting, service quality, or service effectiveness. Administrative violence records, if used, are contextual and do not replace female population as the accessibility denominator.
