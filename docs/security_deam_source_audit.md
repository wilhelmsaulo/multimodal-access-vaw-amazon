# Pará DEAM / specialized police-source audit

## Purpose

Document the evidence base for the security-service layer before any police destination is admitted to the primary accessibility analysis. The objective is to avoid conflating distinct categories such as DEAM, integrated ParáPaz/DEAM units, Sala Lilás and other specialized police spaces.

## Official evidence reviewed

1. **Agência Pará, 8 Mar 2017 — Pro Paz Mulher**
   - Public state-government page lists Belém plus 15 interior DEAM / integrated DEAM units with addresses.
   - This is useful as a historical address baseline, but it is not treated as the current 2026 inventory.
   - URL: https://agenciapara.com.br/noticia/2697/pro-paz-mulher-e-referencia-internacional-na-assistencia-as-vitimas-de-violencia

2. **Agência Pará / Polícia Civil, 4 Nov 2021 — Divisão de Atendimento à Mulher**
   - Reports 18 specialized delegacias in Pará: Belém, Ananindeua, Marituba and 15 in the interior.
   - Provides explicit addresses for Belém and Ananindeua.
   - URL: https://agenciapara.com.br/noticia/32611/divisao-de-atendimento-a-mulher-da-policia-civil-atua-de-forma-efetiva-no-para

3. **Agência Pará / Polícia Civil, 2022 — new Icoaraci DEAM**
   - Reports 21 specialized women-service units: 20 delegacias plus one Sala Lilás in Marituba.
   - This demonstrates that the 18-unit 2021 count is not a stable current inventory.
   - URL: https://agenciapara.com.br/noticia/40337/policia-civil-entrega-novas-unidades-policiais-e-reforca-a-estrutura-da-seguranca-publica

4. **MJSP/SENASP, 8th National Diagnosis of specialized police units for women, published 18 Mar 2024**
   - Current federal official dashboard is the preferred contemporary national cross-check.
   - The public page links to a Power BI dashboard; a reproducible Pará row-level export has not yet been acquired in the repository.
   - URL: https://www.gov.br/mj/pt-br/assuntos/sua-seguranca/seguranca-publica/susp-mulheres/8o-diagnostico-nacional-das-unidades-de-policia-civil-especializadas-o-atendimento-as-mulheres-deam

5. **Pará state sources, 2024–2025**
   - State communications refer to broader networks including DEAM and Sala Lilás and may use totals that are not directly comparable with DEAM-only counts.
   - A state 2025 ODS report refers to maintenance of 18 Delegacias Especializadas; this should not be reconciled mechanically with broader 20/21/24-unit figures without a unit-level list and a definition audit.

## Decision for the study

- The **primary security category will include physical police units whose specialized function for women/VAW is explicitly documented**.
- DEAM and other specialized police spaces will not be pooled merely because they appear in a broad communication count.
- **DEAM Virtual is not a physical destination** and therefore is excluded from travel-time routing; it may be described as contextual remote access.
- **Sala Lilás will not automatically be treated as equivalent to a DEAM**. If included, it must be a separately defined service subtype and analyzed separately unless a defensible equivalence rule is established.
- Historical addresses may be used only for candidate resolution and must be revalidated against a more recent official source before entering routing.
- No police occurrence data (CODEC/CIAC/SEGUP) are used to define demand, incidence, risk, or service validity.

## Current gate

The security layer remains **not materialized for primary routing** until a contemporary unit-level Pará inventory is recovered or reconstructed with explicit provenance. The preferred route is a row-level export from the MJSP 8th Diagnosis dashboard, cross-checked against current Pará government / Polícia Civil / ParáPaz service directories.

## Known current-location evidence useful for cross-checking

Recent official ParáPaz material confirms current integrated women-service locations in the metropolitan region, including Belém, Ananindeua, Icoaraci and the Sala Lilás in Marituba. These records are valuable for address validation but do not by themselves define the statewide DEAM universe.
