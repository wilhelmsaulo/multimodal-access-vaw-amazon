# Core methodological literature

## Pereira et al. (2019)

Pereira, R. H. M.; Braga, C. K. V.; Serra, B.; Nadalin, V. **Desigualdades socioespaciais de acesso a oportunidades nas cidades brasileiras.** Ipea, 2019.

**Role in this project:** cumulative accessibility at travel-time thresholds; comparison among population groups; territorial inequality; mapping of areas with limited opportunity access.

## Tomasiello et al. (2024)

Tomasiello, D. B. et al. **Racial and income inequalities in access to healthcare in Brazilian cities.** *Journal of Transport & Health*, article 101722, 2024.

**Role in this project:** equity analysis; differentiation among service categories and complexity; avoidance of treating all destinations as equivalent.

## Rocha et al. (2023)

Rocha, T. A. H. et al. **River dataset as a potential fluvial transportation network for healthcare access in the Amazon region.** *Scientific Data*, 10, 188, 2023.

**Role in this project:** river network construction; connection of remote communities and services; assignment of river travel parameters; fluvial routing and later integration with road networks.

## Combined methodological lineage

The project adapts Brazilian accessibility-to-opportunities and healthcare-equity methods to VAW services and combines them with an Amazonian fluvial transportation network. The intended advance is an intra-municipal, female-population-weighted, multimodal, door-to-door, operational, and seasonal framework for Pará.
