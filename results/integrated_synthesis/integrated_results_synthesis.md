# Integrated results synthesis — E2SFCA, MCDM and SOM
This synthesis joins already frozen outputs. It does not retrain the SOM, change PROMETHEE-II rankings, or redefine E2SFCA. The integration is descriptive and intended for manuscript interpretation.
## Cross-method interpretation
The reference E2SFCA block contains 4 service types: creas, health, specialized_justice, specialized_security. Within each service, municipal scores are converted to percentiles only to permit a common descriptive scale in the integrated figures. Raw E2SFCA scores are retained in the municipal table. Missing E2SFCA coverage is preserved and never replaced by zero.
The SOM profiles remain neutral socioeconomic-demographic descriptors. PROMETHEE-II remains the frozen multicriteria prioritization result. Consequently, differences in PROMETHEE membership or E2SFCA accessibility across P1–P4 are associations, not causal effects of demographic composition.
## Profile-level integrated pattern
- P1 (n=30): PROMETHEE median rank 118.0; top-quartile share 10.0%; municipalities with complete E2SFCA service coverage 30; median cross-service E2SFCA percentile among complete cases 0.551; concordant PROMETHEE-top-quartile/lower-E2SFCA count 1.
- P2 (n=33): PROMETHEE median rank 89.0; top-quartile share 15.2%; municipalities with complete E2SFCA service coverage 33; median cross-service E2SFCA percentile among complete cases 0.552; concordant PROMETHEE-top-quartile/lower-E2SFCA count 1.
- P3 (n=53): PROMETHEE median rank 56.0; top-quartile share 26.4%; municipalities with complete E2SFCA service coverage 53; median cross-service E2SFCA percentile among complete cases 0.520; concordant PROMETHEE-top-quartile/lower-E2SFCA count 4.
- P4 (n=28): PROMETHEE median rank 36.5; top-quartile share 50.0%; municipalities with complete E2SFCA service coverage 27; median cross-service E2SFCA percentile among complete cases 0.357; concordant PROMETHEE-top-quartile/lower-E2SFCA count 10.

Across individual municipalities, the largest absolute service-specific Spearman association between E2SFCA percentile and PROMETHEE net flow occurs for `specialized_security` (rho=-0.350, n=143). This is descriptive because accessibility-related information is conceptually related to the MCDM construction.

## Interpretation constraints
- Do not interpret P1–P4 as ordinal vulnerability or violence-risk levels.
- Do not interpret E2SFCA missingness as zero accessibility.
- Do not use the mean E2SFCA percentile as a new MCDM criterion or new ranking. It is only a visualization/synthesis aid for municipalities with complete service coverage.
- The rural female share overlaps between SOM and MCDM and must be acknowledged when discussing cross-method associations.
- The integrated result supports triangulation: E2SFCA describes potential service accessibility, PROMETHEE prioritizes municipalities under the frozen multicriteria model, and SOM describes the socioeconomic-demographic contexts in which these patterns occur.
